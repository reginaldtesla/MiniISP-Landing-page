Refund and Cancellation Policy

Refund eligibility and cancellation terms for TesNet hotspot, consultation, and deployment services.

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · tesnet.xyz

On this page

  • Consumer hotspot vouchers
  • Consultation services
  • On-site setup and hardware
  • Disputes and contact

1. Consumer Hotspot Vouchers

  • Non-refundable upon use: Bundle purchases are final once byte consumption has been recorded by the network gateway, except where required by applicable law.
  • Deduction errors: If Mobile Money is debited but credentials fail to activate due to a processing or API timeout, TesNet will investigate and, where verified, aim to credit the missing bundle within 24 hours. If access cannot be restored, a refund may be requested through the provider used for that transaction.

2. Consultation Services

  • Cancellation before engagement: A scheduled consultation may be cancelled up to 24 hours before its start for a refund less any third-party payment processing fees.
  • Completed sessions: Once a consultation has concluded and any agreed documentation or diagrams have been delivered, its fee is non-refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

3. On-Site Setup and Hardware Deployments

  • Procured hardware: Hardware purchased for a client is subject to the supplier's return policy. Opened, mounted, or configured hardware is not refundable unless it is proven defective on arrival and the supplier's terms allow a return or replacement.
  • Installation labor: Deposits allocated to site surveys, engineering labor, or cabling are non-refundable once the relevant on-site work has begun, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • Project termination: If a client cancels a deployment after work has started, charges will be reconciled against work completed and materials that cannot be returned, as set out in the applicable quote or statement of work.

4. Dispute Resolution

Submit refund claims or contract disputes within 7 days of the incident to, with relevant transaction references, invoices, and service agreements to tesnet5532@gmail.com, or call 024 729 5735 or 053 885 0222.

Where a monetary adjustment is approved, it will be requested through the original payment channel where supported. Payment provider processing times vary; allow approximately 3 to 7 business days after initiation for the funds to appear. Never include a Mobile Money PIN, banking password, or full card number in a request.
