How it works

From empty site to working Wi‑Fi

Four clear stages. You own the setup; we help with connectivity and the tech.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Site walkthrough, capacity, open vs paid access, and which backhaul fits.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Get the uplink in place and supply the routers, APs, and materials for your design.

  3. 03

    Configure

    Install and tune coverage — open for everyone, or hotspot packages if you want to charge.

  4. 04

    Go live

    Occupants browse freely, or plug into billing so users pay online — your call.
