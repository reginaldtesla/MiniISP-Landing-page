1. Introduction

Welcome to TesNet (“we,” “our,” or “the Service”), accessible at tesnet.xyz. TesNet provides hotspot voucher provisioning, internet access management, and related network services for student accommodations and other sites. This notice explains information handling for the TesNet website, purchase flow, and hotspot portal.

2. Information We Collect

To process purchases, authenticate hotspot sessions, and provide network access, the relevant TesNet, payment, and network systems may process:

  • Account and contact data: phone number or other contact details submitted during a purchase or support request, and the voucher code or login credentials used by the service. The hotspot portal in this project asks users to enter a voucher code.
  • Network and device data: network identifiers such as device MAC address, assigned IP address, connection timestamps, and active session duration, where recorded by the network gateway or its operator.
  • Bandwidth and accounting data: upload/download usage and package or session limits used to provide and account for the purchased access.
  • Payment transaction data: transaction reference, selected package, amount, and payment status or timestamp needed to confirm a purchase and deliver a voucher.
  • Website data: the theme and external-content choice stored in this browser. The public website does not have a contact form or newsletter signup.

TesNet does not ask users to send Mobile Money PINs, banking passwords, or full card details to us. Payment credentials should be entered only through the payment provider’s official checkout.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate hotspot sessions and provide network access.
  • Apply purchased package limits and account for network usage.
  • Confirm payments, issue or look up vouchers, and help resolve failed or disputed transactions.
  • Monitor service operation, prevent unauthorized access, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests.

4. Payment Processing

Package availability is requested from pay.tesnet.xyz. The TesNet site identifies Paystack and Kowri as payment options; the provider actually used is the one presented in checkout. Payment information is handled under the selected provider’s own terms and privacy notice. TesNet systems receive the transaction and payment-status information needed to confirm the purchase and provide the voucher. We do not receive or store your Mobile Money PIN through this website.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared with payment, hosting, and network infrastructure providers where needed to process a purchase, operate the service, or provide network access. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process in Ghana.

The optional Unsplash images and embedded hotspot preview are withheld until you allow external content. When loaded, image requests go to Unsplash and the preview may request TesNet payment or advertising resources. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp only when you choose them. These providers may receive technical request data under their own privacy terms. This project contains no analytics tags; server-side logging on hosting, payment, or hotspot systems is outside this repository and should be checked with those operators.

6. Data Retention and Security

Network session and accounting records are kept for the operational, support, security, and legal purposes for which they are needed. Specific retention periods depend on the payment, hosting, and network systems in use and must be confirmed with their operators.

Use the secure HTTPS checkout and portal addresses provided by TesNet. The actual production configuration, credential-storage method, and security controls depend on the systems deployed; do not rely on a particular encryption or password-hashing method unless verified by the system operator.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may request access to personal information relating to you and ask for inaccurate information to be corrected. You may also ask about deletion of information that is no longer required, subject to legal, accounting, security, and service-operational obligations. Contact the service operator using the details below. Payment or network records held by independent providers may need to be requested from those providers.
