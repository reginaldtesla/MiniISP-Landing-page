1. Overview

This policy describes cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies used by the TesNet static website and the hotspot pages included in this project. The live payment service, hosting environment, and MikroTik router may use additional storage that is not visible in this codebase. Their operators and deployed configurations should be checked separately.

2. Technologies in Use

The website code in this repository does not directly write HTTP cookies or use session storage. It does use HTML local storage, which saves small values in your browser for later visits. The hotspot status page also uses local storage to retain a voucher-linked package expiry value for its countdown display.

4. External Services and Tracking

The buy page requests package information from pay.tesnet.xyz. The payment flow may involve Paystack or Kowri, depending on the provider shown at checkout. The Unsplash images and embedded hotspot preview are held back until you allow external content; loading them makes requests to Unsplash or TesNet services. These external services may receive technical request information and may use their own cookies or storage.

This repository contains no analytics, advertising pixel, or behavioral profiling tags. That does not establish what may be logged by the hosting provider, payment service, upstream provider, or live router. Review those services' notices for their tracking and storage practices.

5. Network Identifiers

This website does not create a browser cookie containing a hardware MAC address. The hotspot network may process device or connection identifiers, including MAC address or assigned IP address, as part of providing and administering network access. Device operating systems may use randomized MAC addresses; network-side processing is separate from browser cookies and is controlled by the router and network configuration.

6. Managing and Disabling Storage

You can clear this site's local storage through your browser's site settings. This removes saved theme, external-content, and hotspot expiry values; you may need to choose the theme or external-content preference again. You can change the external-content preference using the Privacy choices control in the footer of this page.

Blocking storage may prevent preferences or the hotspot's locally displayed expiry countdown from being retained. Payment or live-router behavior when cookies are blocked depends on those services; consult the provider shown at checkout or the network administrator for details.

7. Legal Information
